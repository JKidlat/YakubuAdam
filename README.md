# YakubuAdam

Welcome to the official source code repository for the professional portfolio website of **Mr. Yakubu Adam**—a motivated toxicologist, lecturer, drug discovery scientist, and public health advocate based in Accra, Ghana. 

This repository hosts a clean, modern, and fully responsive personal website designed to showcase his academic journey, professional milestones, research contributions, public lectures, health columns, and national media appearances.

---

## 🌟 About Mr. Yakubu Adam

Mr. Yakubu Adam is a dedicated academic and researcher specializing in toxicology, molecular biology, and public health. His work bridges the gap between experimental laboratory science and community health advocacy, focusing on:
* **Neuropharmacology & Medicinal Chemistry**: Searching for novel therapeutic agents for neurotoxicity, addiction, and neurodegenerative disorders.
* **Environmental & Occupational Toxicology**: Investigating the health hazards of heavy metal contamination and illegal mining (Galamsey) in Ghana.
* **Public Health Education**: Raising awareness about food safety, cosmetics safety, and public health issues through articles, public lectures, and national television/radio appearances.

## 🖥️ Website Structure & Features

The website is structured into four main pages, each offering detailed insights into Mr. Adam's professional portfolio:

### 1. 🏠 Home Page (`index.html`)
* **Introduction & Bio**: A snapshot of his core research interests and career achievements (7+ years of experience, 4+ published papers).
* **Direct Contact Hub**: A professional contact form allowing prospective research collaborators, students, and institutions to reach out directly via email, phone, or location.
* **Social Connections**: Quick links to his professional networks (LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok).

### 2. 🎓 Education & Experience (`education.html`)
* **Academic Journey Timeline**: Details his academic history:
  * **Master of Public Health (Epidemiology and Disease Control)** - Klintaps University College of Health and Allied Sciences (2026)
  * **MSc Occupational Safety, Health, and Environmental Management (Occupational Toxicology)** - Ghana Institute of Management and Public Administration (GIMPA) (2026)
  * **MPhil Drug Discovery and Toxicology** - University of Cape Coast (2022)
  * **BSc Molecular Biology and Biotechnology** - University of Cape Coast (2018)
* **Professional Experience Timeline**: Lists his roles as a full-time and adjunct lecturer at various universities (Klintaps, Pentecost University, Islamic Nursing Training School, Maranatha, Pharmatrust), as well as his forensic work as a toxicologist at FIND-GH.

### 3. 📚 Lectures & Research (`lectures.html`)
* **Public Lectures**: Highlights of key presentations at national venues and universities focusing on environmental health (e.g., Galamsey-induced kidney failure, lead poisoning, and cosmetic safety).
* **Successful Manuscripts**: A display of active scientific articles currently in peer review (covering subjects like FSP1 inhibitors, reproductive toxicity, AI in molecular biology, and hepatoprotective properties of local herbs).
* **Health & Wellness Articles**: A library of publications written by Mr. Adam for news columns like *Modern Ghana* and *GhanaWeb*, covering public-facing science, food-drug interactions, and cosmetic product safety.

### 4. 📺 Media & Press (`media.html`)
* **National Broadcast Interviews**: A video grid highlighting guest features on national television networks (TV3, GTV, UTV, Metro TV) and radio programs (Peace FM, Starr FM) to discuss pressing toxicology and environmental safety concerns.
* **Interactive Video Modal**: Responsive video previews and direct links to watch full-length interviews on Facebook and YouTube.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

To ensure optimal performance, quick load times, and clean styling, the website is built entirely on a vanilla web stack:
* **HTML5**: Semantic markup structuring the content for accessibility and search engine optimization (SEO).
* **CSS3 (Custom Styling)**: A custom-designed color palette with gold and dark-brown accents representing academic prestige and forensic precision. Features responsive grid and flexbox layouts, smooth hover transitions, and scroll animations.
* **JavaScript (ES6+)**: Custom scripts (`script.js`) handling interactive components like the mobile navigation menu, lazy loading images, scroll-reveal animations, the video modal player, and form validation.
* **Fonts & Icons**: Outfit and Lora fonts from Google Fonts, and iconography from Font Awesome.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

Since this is a lightweight, frontend-only website, you don't need any complex installation steps or server configurations:

1. **Clone the Repository**:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/JKidlat/YakubuAdam.git
   ```
2. **Navigate to the Directory**:
   ```bash
   cd YakubuAdam
   ```
3. **Launch the Site**:
   * **Simple**: Double-click `index.html` to open the site directly in your web browser.
   * **Recommended**: Open the project folder in [Visual Studio Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/) and run it using the **Live Server** extension to ensure smooth routing and video loading. Alternatively, spin up a simple python server in your terminal:
     ```bash
     python -m http.server 8000
     ```
     Then open `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.
